Privacy Policy

Compiler Construction course website and Android app · Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy covers the Compiler Construction course website (compiler-construction-phi.vercel.app) and the Compiler Construction Android app (package com.tahir.compilerconstruction), made for the course taught by Muhammad Tahir Mehmood.

Short version

The app does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

  • Your progress stays on your device. The name you type, completed sessions, quiz and assessment scores, notes, bookmarks and settings (such as light or dark theme) are saved in the app's local storage on your phone or in your browser. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • You can delete all of it at any time with Me → Reset my progress, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Internet use

  • All course content is built into the app, so it works offline.
  • The copy of the app downloaded from GitHub checks a small file on the course website (app-version.json) to tell you when a new version is available. The copy installed from Google Play gets its updates from Google Play instead. Neither sends any information about you.
  • Links to other websites (for example GitHub, or videos) open in your own browser and are covered by those websites' own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the update check and for opening links.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback when you answer questions.

Children

The app is made for university students. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub page: github.com/tahir8203/Compiler-Construction/issues.
